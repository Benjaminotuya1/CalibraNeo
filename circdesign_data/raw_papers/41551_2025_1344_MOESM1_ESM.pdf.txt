We also studied circRNA delivery by liposome (lipo-circRNA) synthesized from 1,2-dioleoyl-3-
trimethylammonium-propane (DOTAP), 1,2-distearoyl-sn-glycero-3-PE (DSPE)-PEG2000, and cholesterol 
(molar ratio: 10: 1: 10)S1 that has great safety. Dynamic light scattering (DLS) showed the lipo-circRNA 
diameter of ~120 nm in PBS, with only slight size increase in 1% FBS (Extended Data Fig. 1a), suggesting 
good stability. We loaded SIINFEKL-encoding circRNA into liposomes at N:P ratios of 1:1, 3:1, and 10:1, 
respectively, (Extended Data Fig. 1b) all of which had ~95% circRNA encapsulation efficiency (amine (N) in 
liposome, phosphate (P) in RNA). As evaluated by the SIINFEKL presentation on DCs, N:P ratio of 3:1 and 
10:1 resulted in most SIINFEKL presentation on DCs (Extended Data Fig. 1c-d). We chose 10:1 for further 
study due to the similarity to Lipofectamine.  
 
DCs are professional APCs that mediate antigen presentation to elicit adaptive immunity. To study 
intracellular lipo-circRNA delivery to DCs, circRNA was labeled with a Cy5-complementary DNA (cDNA) for 
fluorescence tracking of intracellular circRNA. After incubation of DCs with liposomal vs. free circRNA-Cy5 
for a series of time up to 24 h, flow cytometric analysis revealed efficient cell uptake of lipo-circRNA within 1 
h, in contrast to undetectable cell uptake of free circRNA (Extended Data Fig. 1f-g). To further explore 
cellular localization of circRNA, the nuclei and endolysosomes of above-treated DCs were also stained with 
Hoechst33342 and LysoTracker-Green, respectively. Fluorescence confocal laser scanning microscopy 
(CLSM) verified the efficient cell uptake of lipo-circRNA into endolysosomes, and importantly, liposomes 
mediated efficient escape of circRNA from the endolysosome to cytosol where peptide antigens are 
translated (Extended Data Fig. 1e, h). 
 
We then studied lipo-circRNA delivery in Balb/c mice using circRNA modified with near-infrared dye IR800, 
which has great absorption and emission light penetration into tissues, especially superficial lymph nodes. 
Lipo-circRNA and free circRNA were s.c. injected into the mouse footpad, followed by IVIS imaging and 
quantification of fluorescence intensities at the same regions of interest including draining popliteal lymph 
nodes. Impressively, the liposome significantly promoted circRNA delivery to and retention in popliteal lymph 
nodes for up to 8 days when the experiments ended (Extended Data Fig. 2a-b). The long retention of lipo-
circRNA in draining lymph nodes is attributed to the efficient delivery by liposomes as well as the excellent 
stability of circRNA. The efficient circRNA delivery and prolonged circRNA retention are critical to the 
sustained antigen expression in APCs, which is expected to promote adaptive immunomodulation. We then 
dissected the intranodal cell distribution of Cy5-labeled lipo-circRNA upon s.c. injection at tail base, followed 
by lymph node isolation 24 h later, single cell preparation, and flow cytometric analysis of circRNA-Cy5 
signal in cells (Extended Data Fig. 2c-e). Administration of free circRNA resulted in minimal signal in 
inguinal draining lymph nodes after 24 h (Extended Data  Fig. 2c). In contrast, administration of lipo-
circRNA led to a significantly higher signal in inguinal draining lymph nodes compared with the free circRNA 
group (Extended Data Fig. 2c). We studied the circRNA distribution kinetics in representative intranodal Ag-
presenting DC subsets, including CD8+ T-cell-priming cDCs (CD11c+CD11b−CD8+), CD4+ T-cell-priming 
cDCs (CD11c+CD11b+CD4+CD103−CD205+), migratory cDCs (CD11c+CD11b− CD8−CD4−CD103+), and 
F4/80+ macrophages (Extended Data Fig. 2e). Consistent with the above results, Lipo-circRNA promoted 
cellular uptake of antigen, significantly increasing the frequency of CD8+ T-cell-priming cDCs, CD4+ T-cell-
priming cDCs, migratory cDCs, and F4/80+ macrophages. Taken together, these results showed that 
liposomes efficiently deliver circRNA to draining lymph nodes and promote uptake by APCs in draining lymph 
nodes, which are prerequisites for the induction of T cell responses.
