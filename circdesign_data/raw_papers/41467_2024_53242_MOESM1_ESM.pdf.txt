Supplementary Methods 10 
Production of modified linear mRNAs. mRNAs encoding EDIII -Fc and NS1  were 11 
synthesized by in vitro transcription. In brief, plasmid templates covering the tPA signal 12 
sequence and the coding sequences for EDIII-Fc and NS1 were constructed, linearized, 13 
and transcribed in vitro by T7 RNA polymerase. During the transcription process, co -14 
transcriptional capping was employed to add cap analogs to the 5’ end of the mRNA in 15 
real time. Meanwhile, modified nucleobase N1 -Methylpseudouridine-5-Triphosphate 16 
(m1ψ) was incorporated in place of the standard uridine-5-Triphosphate. Following in 17 
vitro transcription, mRNA products were purified through the lithium chloride (LiCl) 18 
method and dissolved in RNase-free water. Finally, purified mRNAs were analyzed by 19 
agarose gel electrophoresis , quantified by spectrophotometry , and subjected to 20 
encapsulation by LNPs.  21
