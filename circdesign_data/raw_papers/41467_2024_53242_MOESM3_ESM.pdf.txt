This study did not involve wild animals.
This study employed three mice models. As for the maternal immunization and neonatal challenge model, the immunization sassy
was performed using female C57BL/6 mice, but the challenge assay involved both female and male pups. As for the adult Ifnar-/-
mice model, we used both male and female mice. As for the challenge assay regarding mouse-adapted DENV2, we used both female
and male 1-day-old ICR mice. Therefore, the mainly findings of this study are not limited to one particular sex. Our primary objective
focused on the general immunogenicity and protective effects of the vaccine across the population, rather than investigating sex-
specific responses. Consequently, although we included both sexes, detailed analysis to determine potential sex-based differences in
immune response were not conducted.
This study did not involve field-collected samples.
