Abstract28
29
Synthetic circular RNA (circRNA) has recently emerged as the next generation30
RNAtechnologyforvaccineanddrugdevelopment,featuringitsuniquenessina31
closed-loop structure and cap-independent translation mechanism. However,32
rationaldesign and optimization of circRNAto improve its stability and protein33
codingpotentialremainschallengingduetolackofstructurepredictiontooland34
sequence design algorithm for endless circular-shaped RNA. In this study, we35
presentedcircDesign,acomputationalapproachtoachieveoptimaldesignofthe36
circRNA with enhanced circularization efficiency, stability, and translatability.37
Using rabies virus glycoprotein (RABV-G) and varicella-zoster virus (VZV)38
glycoprotein gE as the exemplified antigens, we demonstrated that optimized39
circRNAsencodingcorrespondingantigensexhibitedhigherstabilityandprotein40
translation efficiency in vitro. Formulated by proprietary delivery system41
lipopolyplex(LPP),theoptimizedcircRNAvaccinesencodingRABV-GorVZV42
gE antigen further elicited enhanced humoral and cellular in vivo immune43
responsesinmice.Takentogether,thiscircularRNAdesignapproachprovideda44
generalstrategytoleveragethecapabilityofcircRNAasnextgenerationvaccines45
ortherapeutics.46

Introduction73
74
RationalsequencedesignofmessengerRNAs(mRNAs)isofpivotalimportance75
to maximize the potency of mRNA vaccines or therapeutics1. Optimization of76
conventional linear mRNA usually involves 5'- & 3'-untranslated region (UTR)77
and open reading frame (ORF), which could be simply achieved by combining78
naturally occurring UTR pair with codon optimized ORF region2, 3. As79
exemplifiedbyModerna'smRNA-1273and BioNTech'sBNT162b2COVID-1980
mRNA vaccines, the optimized ORF was directly flanked with previously81
screened UTR pair4. Besides, it was reported the functional half-life of mRNA82
dictates its in vivo performance as the translation efficiency and stability of83
mRNA synergistically impact the protein output5, 6. To achieve enduring and84
efficienttranslation,theORFsequenceshouldbeoptimizedtowardsmorestable85
secondarystructurewithoutcompromisingitscodonpreference.86
87
Recent advancement in circRNA demonstrated its potentials as next generation88
vaccinesortherapeuticsowingtoitsuniquenessinendlessstructuredistinctfrom89
linear mRNA7-9. Other than ORF region, synthetic circRNA usually contains90
internal ribosome entry site (IRES) motif upstream of ORF for translation91
initiation10.ThecorrectfoldingofIRESasafunctionalmotiftorecruittranslation92
pre-initiationcomplex(PIC)isaprerequisiteofefficienttranslationofcircRNA11.93
However,duetothelackofstructurepredictiontoolforcircularRNA,alongwith94
insufficient understanding of the crosstalk between IRES and ORF regions on95
circRNA, current sequence optimization of circRNA heavily relied on modular96
screening12.ThisindependentORFoptimizationoncircRNAwithoutconsidering97
the backbone sequence including IRES motif and residual regions as a whole98
introducesmoreuncertaintiesintheprocessofcircRNAsequenceoptimization.99

usageandminimumfreeenergy(MFE).ByassemblyofORFwithIRESmotifas105
well as other functional regions, the positional entropy and ensemble diversity106

circDesign approach for circular RNA design118
119
To implement circDesign for circRNA-based rabies vaccine design, the ORF120
region encoding RABV-G16, 17 was designed such as to achieve efficient and121
prolongedexpression.DifferentfromlinearmRNAdesignwhichiscomposedof122
relatively short functional elements (~ 100 nt in length) including 5'-UTR and123
3'-UTR, artificially constructed circular RNA with protein translation capacity124
usually contains long viral or cellular IRES sequence (500-800 nt in length) for125
cap-independent translation10, 18, 19. More importantly, the IRES region needs to126
be independently folded into a hierarchical structure without interference from127
other sequence as to efficiently recruit translational PIC. Therefore, a key128
consideration in circDesign involves mitigating the cross-interaction of ORF129
region with IRES motif, thus facilitating the formation of functional IRES with130
high confidence. Moreover, following a permuted intron-exon (PIE)131
circularization20, residual exon fragments on circularized RNA is redundant and132
introduced more uncertainties to the function of circRNA as this element may133
interfere with the folding of other elements. As such, precautionsmust be taken134
whenoptimizing the ORF of circRNAsince functionalIRES motif and residual135
exonsarepresentinthesequence.136

541
Inthisstudy,wedevisedanovelalgorithmtool,calledcircDesign,forsimplified542
circular RNA design to improve its stability as well as translatability. The543
usefulnessofthiscircDesignplatformwasvalidatedusingRABV-GandVZVgE544
antigens against the benchmark designs which were designed via conventional545
CAI-oriented method. Here we present evidence supporting the enhanced546
stability and translation efficiency of circDesign optimized sequence which547
contributed to considerably stronger in vivo immune responses. We speculated548
that the sustained expression of antigens at high efficiency achieved greater549
antigen-specific immune activations, leading to distinguished vaccine-elicited550
humoral and cellular immune responses. Overall, these data demonstrated an551
approachfor rationalcircRNA design in supportof next generationvaccine and552
therapeuticdevelopment.553
554
The major limitation of this study lies in the fact that we performed extensive555
search on specific ORF sequences without further optimization of other556
functionalregionsofcircRNAsuchasIRESmotif.Tothebestofourknowledge,557
thedenovodesignofanefficientIRESmotifremainschallengingasthesevirally558
orcellularlyderivedIRESmotifsareusuallysensitivetosequencemanipulation559
including deletion, mutation, or insertion. The different domains of IRES560
sequence cohesively function to recruit PIC and assemble the intact ribosome561
complex only when these domains are well-folded. Therefore, we did not562
incorporate the modulation of IRES region in the circDesign at current stage.563
Providedthatthestructure-to-activityofIRESwereelucidatedatmolecularlevel,564
it might be possible to realized bottom-up search or optimization of IRES565
sequenceincircDesignalgorithm.566
567
Other than IRES-driven translation from circRNA, N6-methyladenosine (m6A)568
modification upstream of ORF has been reported to facilitate the translation as569
well as the stability44, 45. If chemically modified nucleotides were incorporated570
into circRNA, it would pose difficulties on our circDesign algorithm as the571
structure prediction based on unmodified nucleotides is not reliable. Given that572
the energy model of chemically modified nucleotide is available, we would be573
abletodoextensivestructurecomparisonforoptimalsequencedesign.574
